👋 Meet the Founder

Arjun Nair, Redmond High School

Arjun founded Wavelength STEM after two years of teaching hands-on science and math at Horace Mann Elementary. His work in youth STEM education and community service — including organizing a book drive that brought roughly 5,000 books to underpriviledged children — was recognized by the Lake Washington School District and helped earn him a Presidential Service Award. Wavelength STEM is the next chapter of that same commitment: bringing real, hands-on science to more elementary students across Redmond.

Is this a real, credible program?
Yes — it's led by a Redmond High School student with two years of hands-on classroom teaching experience at Horace Mann, a Presidential Service Award for youth service, and mentorship connections with real scientists and research organizations.
What will my child actually learn?
Hands-on physics, how scientists collect real data, and — for kids who want to go further — how to design and present their own science fair project, following Washington's real state science fair pathway.
Is there any cost or fundraising involved?
No. This program does not charge fees and does not solicit donations of any kind.
How big are the groups?
Small and hands-on — every session is designed so kids actually get to touch, build, and try things themselves, not just watch.
